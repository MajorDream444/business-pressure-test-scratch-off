Participant responsibilities

Working agreement — review draft

This outlines the reciprocal commitments for a Blueprint. A scoped implementation engagement needs a separate signed statement of work.

We agree the work

Record the service, included conversation, deliverables, input deadline, review date, delivery date and contact person. The participant and provider both receive a copy. Scope and payment are linked to the order and terms version.

When something stalls

We send a factual reminder and ask what is needed. If required input is missing, only the affected work pauses and we propose a revised schedule. We do not infer emotional causes or penalize someone for declining optional reflection.

Fair accountability

Both parties communicate changes and meet the commitments they accept. No hidden fines, unilateral new fees or unapproved scope. Repeated missed commitments can lead to a mutually documented pause or end to the engagement, with completed work and refunds handled under reviewed terms.

Recording and materials

Google Meet conversations are not recorded by default. Recording, transcription and processing by AI require disclosed purpose and suitable consent. Private material stays private; publication or resale requires separate rights and consent.
