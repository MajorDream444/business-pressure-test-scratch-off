Refunds, cancellations and rescheduling

Proposed policy — review required

These are proposed customer-friendly terms, not yet activated. Purchases remain disabled pending owner review and publication of effective terms.

Before work starts

Request cancellation at artmobagi@gmail.com before the review begins and before agreed custom preparation starts for a full refund. We will not start billable preparation without explaining it and obtaining your agreement.

After work starts

If you cancel after an agreed part of the service is delivered, we explain what has been completed and propose a proportionate refund for undelivered work. We do not automatically confiscate the entire fee. Mandatory cancellation or withdrawal rights take precedence.

Rescheduling

Proposed default: request at least 24 hours before the session. One reschedule is included. If you miss the session or give less notice, contact us to agree a workable next step; there is no automatic fine. Any further charge requires a separate written agreement.

Provider cancellation or non-delivery

If we cancel, choose a replacement time or a refund for undelivered service. If an agreed deliverable is missing, contact us so we can correct it or agree an appropriate refund. We acknowledge requests within two business days; approved refunds are initiated within five business days. Bank processing may take longer.

How to request

Send the email used at checkout and order reference. Do not send card numbers. Refunds go through the original payment method. This policy does not restrict statutory rights or chargeback rights. Implementation and subscriptions require their own expressly agreed policies.
