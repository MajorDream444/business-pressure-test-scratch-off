Your answers belong to your journey.

You can see your results without giving us your name or email. Answers stay in this browser tab until you choose to save them or close the tab.

When you choose to save

AMA Solutions Corporation, providing MAIM services, stores the answers, contact details, business context, results, and consent you submit in MAIM’s Airtable client records. We use these to provide your results, understand the support you requested, and respond to that request. Saving does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Services that handle the request

Vercel hosts the app and processes web requests. Airtable stores saved submissions. Resend processes results emails when email delivery is enabled. These services may process data in countries outside your own. Hosting providers may collect standard request logs.

When you purchase

Stripe handles checkout and payment details. MAIM stores the confirmed order reference, buyer contact details, amount, currency and payment status in Airtable to arrange and deliver your blueprint. We do not store card numbers. Purchases do not subscribe you to marketing or authorize research reuse.

Conversations and appointments

Google Calendar and Google Meet handle scheduled conversations when you book. We use artmobagi@gmail.com for scheduling and replies. Sessions are not recorded by default; any recording, transcription or AI processing requires a separately explained purpose and suitable consent. Supabase has an isolated MAIM database foundation. Website-to-database synchronization, authenticated workspace access and vault ingestion are not yet active; no assessment or media is transferred there by this release.

Your control

Do not include passwords, financial account numbers, health records, or private details about other people. To request a copy, correction, or deletion of a saved submission, contact artmobagi@gmail.com with the email used for the test. We may verify that the request is yours before acting.

We keep saved results while responding to your request or maintaining an active client relationship. You can request deletion when you no longer want us to keep them, subject to records we must retain.
