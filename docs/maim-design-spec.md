# MAIM diagnostic design and scope

Reusable MAIM module. This iteration is the Business Pressure Test, not a client workspace and not the MAIM knowledge-session landing page. The canonical brand grammar applies; schedule and course modules from the main MAIM landing-page specification are outside this diagnostic's scope.

Sources: Major-AI-OS DESIGN.md v2.3, design-tokens.json, MAIM client workspace system contract and intact official seal. Palette: #050505 black, #111111 surface, #F7F4EA ivory, #D8AF55 gold; tri-bar #1F7A3A / #D8AF55 / #006BB6; #F58426 on the main action. Local Manrope body and strong editorial headings; Cormorant italic for the orbit's quiet Clarity moment. Serif is restrained. Content uses open bands and hairlines rather than nested card stacks.

Generated reference: landing, quiz, and result concepts with a calm grid, orbit, numbered answer list and evidence rows. Intended deviations: use the official seal in the reserved logo position; retain the actual ten questions and five answer choices rather than concept filler; use Print / save PDF for the browser's real print capability; remove invented navigation and severity pills; render recommendations from actual answers. Mobile removes the decorative orbit and keeps the primary task readable.

Screens: landing → ten questions with back/review → result map → optional save form → saved/email-failed/disabled states. Keyboard focus follows the heading when the quiz step changes; native radio controls and visible focus indicators support keyboard use. Reduced motion is respected. Results stay available without contact capture or provider settings.

One owner-reviewed next action. No guaranteed revenue claim, unsupported build credit, fake checkout, testimonial, or live service status. Score is described as self-reported operating structure. No email or newsletter signup is required to see results.

Maintenance: shared/diagnostic.ts owns answer validation and scoring. shared/email-template.ts owns escaped email output. api/submit.ts owns saving and delivery. api/challenge.ts owns capture capability and signed form challenges. server.ts reuses both endpoints locally. src/App.tsx and index.css own presentation. Owner: Major / AMA. Trigger: voluntary quiz and consented save. Failure: visible retry, stable upsert reference, separate saved and email statuses. Handoff: README.md and staging validation notes.
